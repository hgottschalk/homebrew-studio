# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in **Homebrew Recipe Studio** a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of brewing experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other homebrewers and developers
* Being respectful of differing brewing methods (All-Grain, BIAB, Extract, No-Chill, Pressure Fermentation), opinions, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the open-source homebrewing community

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer (**Harold Gottschalk — `hgottschalk@softbrew.com`**). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
